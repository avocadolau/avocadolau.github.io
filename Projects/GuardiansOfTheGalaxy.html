GUARDIANS OF THE GALAXY

ART DIRECTOR

PIXEL PLAYGROUND

FEB 2023 — MAY 2025

3D Top-down shooter roguelike in a custom Game Engine with C++ and OpenGL. The game features a stilized comic art style.

MISSION LOG

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Every year, students from CITM develop a videogame with an own game engine. This time, as Pixel Playground, we have developed a rogelike top-down shooter with Guardians of the Galaxy as theme and it was developed in the short period of time of 3 months.

The game features stilized comic art style. Due to the custom engine, assets were developed in low poly to reach a better optimization.
